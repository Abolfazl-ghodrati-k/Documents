In previous chapters, we explored the fundamental concepts and traditional techniques used in recommender systems. We now extend this foundation by examining the role of **Deep Learning in Recommender Systems**.

Over recent years, deep learning has become one of the most influential areas of artificial intelligence, producing significant advances across numerous domains. Its ability to learn complex patterns and representations from large datasets has also created new opportunities for improving recommendation models.

This chapter introduces the motivations for applying deep neural networks to recommender systems, examines several important neural architectures, and explains how these approaches can improve recommendation and prediction quality.

The chapter focuses on three principal objectives:

1. **Understanding the Motivation for Deep Learning:** Examining why deep neural networks are increasingly used in recommender systems and how they can address certain limitations of traditional recommendation techniques.
2. **Exploring Deep Learning Architectures:** Introducing different neural network architectures used in recommendation tasks and explaining the fundamental ideas behind their operation and their potential contributions to recommendation quality.
3. **Identifying Limitations and Challenges:** Discussing the disadvantages, practical difficulties, and computational challenges associated with deep learning-based recommendation approaches.

### Scope and Prerequisites

**The material in this chapter is intended to provide a conceptual overview and introductory understanding of deep learning-based recommender systems.**

A comprehensive understanding of the methods discussed may require additional prerequisites, particularly in neural networks, optimization, and deep learning.

The purpose of this course is not to provide a complete introduction to deep learning or an exhaustive mathematical treatment of advanced neural recommendation architectures. Such topics extend beyond the scope of the course and require dedicated study.

Readers interested in developing a deeper understanding of these subjects are encouraged to consult specialized educational resources, including:

- [Deep Learning Jump Course](https://quera.org/college/landpage/15508/deep-learning), offered by Quera College.
- Other specialized courses covering neural networks and deep learning.
- Academic textbooks, including [*Deep Learning* by Christopher M. Bishop and Hugh Bishop](https://www.bishopbook.com/).

The following sections progressively introduce the structures, underlying principles, and applications of selected deep learning methods in recommender systems.

By examining these approaches, readers will develop a broader understanding of how deep learning can contribute to more effective recommendation models, while also recognizing the limitations and practical considerations associated with their implementation.
